# Brief-intake worked repairs

All cases are **hypothetical**. Failure descriptions are simulated editorial
judgments, not observed generated-media results.

## A hybrid brief reduced to a genre preset

- **Brief:** A bright noir comedy in a bakery. Viewers should notice the baker
  concealing a missing pastry while the inspector remains oblivious.
- **Initial proposal:** Neon shadows, fearful acting, handheld push-in, teal-orange.
- **Simulated failure:** The palette contradicts the bright request; camera motion
  distracts from who can see the empty tray. Genre has displaced the joke.
- **Smallest repair:** Keep cheerful morning light. Use a stable two-shot where
  the baker slides a receipt over the empty tray while answering the inspector.
  Hold long enough for the viewer to see the concealment before the inspector turns.
- **Tradeoff:** Less overt noir iconography; stronger audience complicity. Borrow
  noir's withheld information without copying its familiar lighting treatment.
- **Acceptance:** A viewer can identify the hidden fact, who knows it, and why the
  framing reveals it, while all supplied tonal constraints remain intact.

## Two treatments that are only palette variants

- **Brief:** Explore ways to make first-time community-dinner guests feel welcome.
- **Initial proposals:** Warm handheld dinner footage; cool handheld dinner footage.
- **Simulated failure:** Both use the same idea and viewpoint. Choosing a color
  temperature does not help the user choose a creative direction.
- **Smallest repair:** A: guest-eye doorway view as diners make room, emphasizing
  personal invitation. B: overhead empty table accumulating each person's object
  and a final extra place setting, emphasizing collective contribution.
- **Tradeoff:** A needs legible ensemble performance; B loses facial connection
  but offers a simpler visual pattern. Neither is selected automatically.
- **Acceptance:** Each treatment explains a different emotional mechanism and a
  concrete feasibility tradeoff; both serve the same audience reaction.

## A treatment with no camera or light stance

- **Brief:** A 20-second ad for a rooftop restaurant. Viewers should feel the
  place is a destination worth dressing up for.
- **Initial proposal:** "Cinematic, warm, elegant. Static wide shots of the
  terrace." No camera or light stance beyond the mood words.
- **Simulated failure:** The proposal names a feeling but gives the production no
  way to show it. Static wides at one eye-level framing make the destination feel
  like a listing photo.
- **Smallest repair:** Keep the warmth and add a stance tied to the idea: a rising
  move from the street to the roof as the reveal, a low angle on the guests
  arriving as the invitation, and dusk practicals as the single light source, with
  an energy level of standard.
- **Tradeoff:** More facts to carry through the shot plan; the reveal now has a
  visible mechanism.
- **Acceptance:** The proposal names a camera stance, a light source and an energy
  level, each tied to the intended reaction, and any static choice states why.

## An ad brief that leaves sound and structure to the edit

- **Brief:** A 30-second wordless spot for a rain jacket. Viewers should feel
  the jacket turns a storm into play.
- **Initial proposal:** One 30 s pass following a cyclist through the city in
  the rain, native audio with "no music, rain only", warm teal grade.
- **Simulated failure:** The rushed hood action and arrival obscure how the
  storm becomes play; audio ownership is unresolved, and native exclusions
  cannot guarantee rain-only sound; the grade has no stated creative purpose or selection.
- **Smallest repair:** Give the hood action and arrival readable time, with
  coverage chosen for the transition from discomfort to play. Equal lengths,
  a held frame or an optional standout image are available, not quotas.
  Plan rain sound closing in under the hood, then opening into splashes at play.
  Ask about audio ownership. When separate sound control is wanted, propose
  `picture_only` and build rain, footsteps and any requested score in post;
  preserve requested native diegetic sound in this wordless scene.
  Propose original colours for this treatment because the
  rain and practical light already carry it; delegated authorship could instead
  propose a justified custom look. List the logo end card as a post
  overlay.
- **Tradeoff:** A post sound pass needs measured cue timing and listening.
  Any additional generated clips require authorization.
- **Acceptance:** The proposal names the sequence structure, the audio route
  with its reason, the look default, and the on-screen copy as post overlays.
