# Formats

Read [the entrypoint](../SKILL.md). Each format lists its engine, a
three-scene shape, and the kind of physical proof that makes the turn read on a
phone screen. Pick by audience first, then by what one location and two or
three characters can stage. Local flavor comes from
[market adaptation](market-adaptation.md), not from the format.

| Format | Engine | Hook → Turn → Cliffhanger | Proof on screen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Billionaire or CEO romance | Power gap, contract or secret identity | Collision in a status space → a humiliating offer or fake-relationship deal → she already knew who he was | Contract envelope, ring, a name card |
| Hidden heir or identity reveal | A mistreated helper, spouse or child is secretly the heir | Accused of theft or failure → the lawyer, relative or elder reveals the will or parentage → the villain hints at a deeper secret | Sealed will, empty jewelry box, family crest |
| Revenge and rebirth | A wronged hero returns stronger, or lives a second chance | Public betrayal → the hero's changed plan lands → the enemy realizes the hero remembers | Old photo, scar, ledger, calendar date |
| In-law and family conflict | A newcomer against a controlling family | Humiliation at a family meal → an ally or hidden proof flips the table → the elder changes sides | Gift box, recipe, family album |
| Mafia, alpha or dark romance | Dangerous protector, forbidden attraction | Wrong room, wrong night → the protector claims her in public → a rival's threat | Signet ring, key, jacket over shoulders |
| Betrayal and legal wife or husband | A spouse finds proof of an affair | Finds the clue → public confrontation → the other party produces a counter-proof | Matching ring, receipt, photo |
| Switched at birth | A poor child is the rich family's real child | Class humiliation → a witness confesses → the winner lies about the result | Birthmark, DNA envelope, bracelet |
| Campus and first love | Friendship, rivalry, first love | Public embarrassment → the loyal friend stands up → a betrayal inside the group | Confession letter, borrowed jacket |
| Thriller and mystery | A normal person notices something wrong | Odd detail → a stranger's slip → the detail was a warning | A key, a locked door, a photo with one face crossed out |
| Horror and folk supernatural | Home, elders' warnings, night | A ritual ignored → a sleepwalker or reflection wrong → a tell only the audience sees | A shadow, a mirror, an offering |
| Comedy and mistaken identity | Two people confused for others | Public mix-up → escalating cover story → the real person walks in | Wrong name tag, swapped bag |
| Historical and fantasy | Court, clan or kingdom intrigue | Banquet accusation → an artifact proves innocence → a larger plot is hinted | Seal, jade token, sleeve-hidden letter |
| True-to-life sacrifice | Poverty, illness, migration, injustice | A small daily struggle → the sacrifice is revealed → the cost lands | Receipt, hospital bill, packed bag |

## Choosing for AI production

- Prefer formats whose proof is a single object or mark that one close-up can
  show.
- Formats with one location per scene and two or three characters hold
  identity best.
- Avoid on-screen text proofs such as phone screens or documents with legible
  writing; show the object and let dialogue name its content.
- For batches, vary format, tone and location across episodes.

## Source basis

Format names are genre conventions drawn from public reporting on vertical
drama platforms and from the Filipino market pack's research. Recheck current
performance for the target market before making market claims.
