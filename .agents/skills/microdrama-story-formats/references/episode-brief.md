# Episode Brief

Read [the entrypoint](../SKILL.md). This template illustrates the default
formula, not a mandatory shape. For a confirmed script or custom format,
replace S1/S2/S3 with the approved scenes and timing, retain its cast and
ending, and mark dialogue or cliffhanger fields not applicable when absent.
Do not invent story material to fill fields. A production orchestrator treats
accepted dialogue as locked wording; a new draft is not automatically approved.

## Template

```text
## <slug> — "<Title>" (<format>)

Market: <country or region>; dialogue language: <language, register>; subtitles: <language>.
Format: <format + key devices>.
Logline: <one sentence>.
Cliffhanger question: <the question the episode leaves open>.

Cast (≤3): **<Name>** (<age>, <look>, <wardrobe>, always-worn: <items>). ...
Location(s): <name> — <layout, landmarks, light, time of day>.
Props: <story-critical objects; note "no legible text">.

S1 Hook (<N> s): <action beats>.
- <Speaker>: {<exact line>}
S2 Turn (<N> s): <action, confrontation or reveal, proof shown in close-up>.
- <Speaker>: {<exact line>}
S3 Cliffhanger (<N> s): <action>; hold on <character>'s face.
- <Speaker>: {<exact line>}

Subtitles: "<source phrase>" = "<translation>"; ...
Limits: fictional cast, no real brands or legible signage, non-injurious conflict.
```

## Worked example

Illustrative, not yet produced.

```text
## the-wrong-floor — "The Wrong Floor" (billionaire romance, secret identity)

Market: urban Brazil; dialogue language: Brazilian Portuguese, casual
register; subtitles: English.
Format: contract romance + secret identity.
Logline: A temp worker mistaken for the CEO's fiancée learns the CEO is the
quiet man who fixed her bike last week.
Cliffhanger question: Why does he already know her name?

Cast: **Marina** (26, temp worker, ponytail, grey blazer, lanyard).
**Rafael Duarte** (34, CEO, navy suit, no tie, always-worn: steel watch).
**Dona Celia** (58, board chair, silver bob, emerald jacket).
Location: executive floor lobby — glass walls, a long marble reception desk,
city skyline behind; late-morning white light.
Props: cream envelope with a gold seal (no legible text); a worn bicycle
bell in Marina's tote.

S1 Hook (15 s): Marina steps off the wrong elevator and Dona Celia grabs her
hand, announcing her as the fiancée.
- Dona Celia: {Finalmente! Esta é a noiva do Rafael.}
- Marina: {Desculpa, senhora, eu sou só a temporária.}
S2 Turn (20 s): Rafael appears, sees Marina, and plays along; he slides the
envelope across the desk.
- Rafael: {Três meses. Você finge, eu pago.}
S3 Cliffhanger (15 s): Marina pulls the bicycle bell from her tote; Rafael's
face changes.
- Marina: {Eu já te conheço, Rafael.}
Hold on Rafael: caught, then a small smile.

Subtitles: "Finalmente! Esta é a noiva do Rafael." = "At last! This is
Rafael's fiancée."; "Três meses. Você finge, eu pago." = "Three months. You
pretend, I pay."
```
