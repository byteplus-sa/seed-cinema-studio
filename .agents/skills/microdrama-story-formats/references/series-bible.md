# Series Bible

Read [the entrypoint](../SKILL.md). Write the bible before any episode brief
for a connected series of 6-10 episodes.

## Template

```text
# <Series title>

Market and audience: <country or region, language, register, age band, platform>.
Format and tone: <format, tone, one-line promise>.
Premise: <two sentences>.
Season question: <the question the final cliffhanger answers>.

## Cast (canon)
**<Name>** — <age, role, look, wardrobe, always-worn items, want, secret>.
Relationships: <who owes, hides or hurts whom>.

## Locations (canon)
<name> — <layout, landmarks, light, time-of-day anchors>. One door, one
staircase, one window layout per location; do not change it between episodes.

## Episode ladder
| Ep | Title | Format device | Hook | Turn | Cliffhanger | New information |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Continuity ledger
- Objects that change state (broken, soaked, locked, missing) and the episode
  where they change.
- Facts the audience knows that characters do not.
- Dialogue lines that must be paid off later.

## Limits
Fictional cast, no real brands or legible signage, non-injurious conflict.
```

## Rules

- Each episode ends on a question the next episode answers within its first
  five seconds.
- Escalate stakes by reveal, not by spectacle.
- Every location has one dedicated reference plate before production; every
  continuity-critical object has its own prop plate.
